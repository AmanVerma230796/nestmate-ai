1. Overview
NestMate AI is a mobile-first app for urban Indian renters that matches verified, lifestyle-compatible flatmates via swipe discovery and a 0–100% compatibility score, then converts matched pairs into daily users of an AI-assisted household manager (chores, staff, bills).

2. Problem & thesis
Finding a flatmate and living with one are both broken, and no product owns both halves.
Pre-move-in: flatmate discovery runs through unverified Facebook/WhatsApp groups flooded with brokers. Renters decide under time pressure with zero data on lifestyle fit (diet and kitchen rules, sleep, cleanliness, guests, WFH noise).
Post-move-in: friction over dishes, guests, maid/cook pay and bills festers because raising it directly feels awkward. Tools are fragmented across WhatsApp, Splitwise and whiteboards.
The structural trap: pure matching apps suffer success churn — users delete the app the day a lease is signed. Pure chore apps have cold-start friction. Pairing them solves both, if matched users actually cross into the household product.
Thesis: verified, compatibility-scored swipe matching acquires users at peak urgency (<7 days to match); an AI household OS retains them across the 11-month lease.
The central bet of this PRD: everything depends on the Discovery → Home Mode bridge. Matching mechanics are well understood; whether matched pairs migrate from WhatsApp into Home Mode is unproven. v2.0 therefore validates the bridge before building the full Home OS (see §10).

7. Core user journey
One funnel from signup to retained household; step 6 is the bridge every upstream step feeds.
Verify — work email, LinkedIn, student ID or govt ID; unverified users cannot swipe or be seen.
Quiz — 10–15 lifestyle questions; dealbreakers first (diet/kitchen, smoking, budget, gender preference), soft habits after (sleep, cleanliness, WFH, social vibe).
Swipe — deck pre-filtered by hard dealbreakers within the corridor radius, ranked by compatibility score; score breakdown visible on every card.
Match & chat — mutual swipe opens a 24-hour icebreaker window with suggested prompts from the two profiles' top synergy and friction points (template-driven, not LLM).
Move-in handshake — either user taps "We're moving in together"; both confirm.
Bridge (F-06) — chat detects move-in discussion or handshake and surfaces the 1-tap "Generate our house rules & split sheet" CTA. This is the make-or-break step.
Home Mode — AI drafts the House Constitution from both quiz vectors; flatmates edit and approve; chore rotation, staff schedule and split ledger activate.
Daily loop — chore checkoffs, maid attendance, bill splits with UPI deep links; 4–6 opens/week sustains retention through the lease.
8. Key functional requirements
Trust & verification
Verification gate before any profile is visible or can swipe; student-ID and freelancer paths (GST/portfolio + govt ID) sit beside work email/LinkedIn so legitimate non-corporate users are not excluded.
Max 2 active listings per account; 3 verified broker reports auto-freeze an account pending review.
Phone numbers never shown publicly; chat stays in-app until users share contacts themselves.
Matching
Hard dealbreakers enforced as SQL filters before ranking — a dealbreaker mismatch never appears in the deck.
Compatibility score is deterministic and auditable (weighted scoring in TypeScript/SQL, sub-50 ms); every score shows its per-dimension breakdown.
Geo-radius matching via PostGIS, scoped to launch corridors.
Home Mode (built after bridge validation)
House Constitution: 6–8 editable rules drafted from quiz vectors + flat specs; activates only after every flatmate approves.
Chore rotation fair across 2–4 flatmates; supports domestic staff schedules (maid, cook, water delivery).
Ledger tracks splits and generates UPI deep links; the app never holds funds. Reminders are scheduled and neutral ("Rent split due Friday"), not peer-to-peer nags.
